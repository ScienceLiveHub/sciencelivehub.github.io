WHAT IS A NANOPUBLICATION?

The smallest citable unit of science

A nanopublication is one clear statement (a claim, a dataset, a replication verdict) signed with its author's ORCID, with its source and a permanent identifier. People and AI can find it, check it and cite it. No more invisible work buried in PDFs.
