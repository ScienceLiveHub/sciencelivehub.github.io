Every funding decision rests on published results. Some have been independently
checked, many have not, and a few are cited over and over without ever having been
reproduced. Science Live makes that difference visible.

## What we offer

## Why it is trustworthy

Every replication is a signed, permanent chain of nanopublications, from the original
claim to the data, the method, and the verdict, with the name of everyone involved.
Nothing is a black box, and anyone can check our work. [Read how we keep paid work
independent](/request-a-replication/#our-commitment-to-independence).
