What can be replicated

We are strongest on computational and data-driven studies: results that can be rerun from published data and code, or rebuilt from independent data. Our expertise is in Earth, climate, and environmental science, and we take on other fields case by case. Laboratory experiments that need new physical measurements are out of scope.

Our commitment to independence

Science Live is operated by VitenHub AS, which also delivers the paid replications on this page. To keep the evidence trustworthy whoever pays for it:

  • Same rules for everyone. Paid and community replications are published with the same templates and the same level of openness.
  • Every paid replication is published, whatever the outcome. That includes its limits, what does not work and what could not be replicated. A replication that refutes or qualifies a claim is published just like one that confirms it.
  • Your private data stays private. Public replications use open or independent datasets. If you want the analysis run on data you cannot share, we hand you the full, documented pipeline to run yourself; nothing about your data is published.
  • Who asked is public. Every request is signed by the person who made it, and the replication links back to it.
  • Anyone can challenge it. Every replication can itself be replicated or disputed, by anyone.
  • Other providers welcome. Research groups and companies that want to offer replications on these terms can contact us.
