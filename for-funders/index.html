For funders and investors

Know which results your money rests on, which are contested, and where the gaps are.

Every funding decision rests on published results. Some have been independently checked, many have not, and a few are cited over and over without ever having been reproduced. Science Live makes that difference visible.

What we offer

State of the evidence

For a field, a program, or a portfolio: which key claims have been replicated, with what verdict, which are contested, and which have never been checked.

Gaps and emerging interest

Open replication requests show where researchers and practitioners doubt the evidence, and where interest is growing, before it shows up in citations.

Sponsored replication campaigns

Fund the replications your community is asking for. Results are always published openly, and your requests stay linked to every replication they lead to.

Due diligence on key claims

Before you invest in a program or a company, have the handful of results it depends on independently checked.

Why it is trustworthy

Every replication is a signed, permanent chain of nanopublications, from the original claim to the data, the method, and the verdict, with the name of everyone involved. Nothing is a black box, and anyone can check our work. Read how we keep paid work independent.
